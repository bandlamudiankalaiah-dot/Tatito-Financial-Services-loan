Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Key details about loan limits, interest methods, and investing.

How does Tatito calculate loan interest?

All loans at Tatito Financial Services utilize the standard RBI-approved reducing balance formula. As your monthly EMIs repay portions of the principal, subsequent interest is computed exclusively on the remaining balance.

Where is the Tatito branch located in Kadapa?

Our central operational hub is located at Door No. 4/320, Trunk Road, Near 7-Roads Junction, Kadapa, YSR District, Andhra Pradesh - 516001. Walk-in consultations are open Monday through Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

What are the minimum amounts to start an investment?

Mutual fund Systematic Investment Plans (SIPs) start from just ₹500 per month. Stock market simulations allow fractional purchase exploration without real capital risk.

Is my gold safe when taking a Gold Loan?

Yes. Pledged gold ornaments are assayed under certified digital scales and securely stored in bank-grade high-security safes at our Kadapa branch, protected by round-the-clock CCTV surveillance and 100% full insurance coverage.
